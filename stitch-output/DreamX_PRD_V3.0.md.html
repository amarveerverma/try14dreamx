Executive summary DreamX is an India-first, outcome-accountable Career Success Marketplace. It helps students, fresh graduates, early-career professionals and career switchers make measurable progress toward career goals by connecting them with vetted mentors and structured career services. DreamX is not merely a directory of mentor profiles or a marketplace for unstructured calls. Its product loop is: 1. Understand the user's career goal and current readiness. 2. Identify the most important gaps using an evidence-informed assessment. 3. Recommend relevant services and mentors. 4. Enable direct booking or a guided Smart Match request. 5. Deliver a clearly scoped mentor session. 6. Provide a structured service-specific report and action plan. 7. Update the user's career journey and readiness history. 8. Recommend the next useful action without promising a job or placement outcome. ### Product pillars 1. **Career services marketplace:** standardised, clearly described 1:1 services with transparent prices, durations and deliverables. 2. **Career readiness and guided journey:** an eight-dimension readiness assessment, evidence coverage, progress history and deterministic next-step recommendations. 3. **Trust and mentor quality:** separate identity verification, professional/experience verification and service-specific approval. 4. **Booking and Smart Match:** reliable availability, atomic booking, mentor acceptance and explicit student choice when a match cannot be found. 5. **Memberships and bundles:** optional recurring benefits and a simple custom bundle discount, without discount stacking. 6. **Operations and accountability:** reports, support, cancellations, refunds, disputes, notifications, audit logs and measurable marketplace quality. ### MVP principle The MVP is deterministic and operationally focused. It should prove that users will pay for specific, useful career services and that DreamX can deliver a trustworthy end-to-end experience. LLM co-pilot features, AI tools and credit-wallet monetisation belong to Phase 2. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 2. Product vision, mission and positioning ## 2.1 Vision Make practical, trustworthy career guidance accessible and measurable at every important stage of a person's career journey. ## 2.2 Mission Help career seekers identify what to improve, access credible human guidance, receive actionable feedback and track progress through a structured platform. ## 2.3 Positioning **DreamX --- an outcome-accountable Career Success Marketplace.** DreamX combines: - Human mentor expertise. - Defined service outcomes and deliverables. - Evidence-informed career readiness. - Transparent booking and refund rules. - A guided journey that connects one service to the next useful action. ## 2.4 Initial India launch domains The initial India MVP domains are founder-selected launch hypotheses: 1. Software Engineering 2. Data Science / AI 3. Product Management These are the initial focus areas for mentor recruitment, service relevance and pilot learning. Their order is not a validated market-demand ranking. DreamX should expand into additional domains only after the initial areas demonstrate adequate mentor supply, student demand and reliable service fulfilment. ## 2.5 What DreamX may promise DreamX may promise: - A clearly described service. - A stated duration and deliverable. - Access to a mentor who has passed the required verification and service approval. - Transparent booking, cancellation and support processes. - A structured report within the stated service SLA. - Progress tracking based on recorded assessment evidence. DreamX must not promise: - A guaranteed job, internship, salary, offer, shortlist or interview pass. - A guaranteed mentor match before a mentor has accepted. - A particular outcome rate unless it is supported by valid, clearly defined data. - A payout, refund or settlement time that the payment provider has not confirmed. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 3. Memberships, discounts and bundles ## 7.1 Membership plans Plan Monthly Annual Service discount ------- ------------ --------------------------- ------------------ Free ₹0 --- 0% Pro ₹199/month ₹1,788/year, paid upfront 5% Elite ₹399/month ₹4,188/year, paid upfront 10% Annual prices are the approved plan prices. It replaces the older fixed four-session/15%-off/24-hour roll-forward proposal. The product must define eligible service combinations, redemption order, booking expiry, partial cancellation, partial refund and mentor-mix behaviour in configuration before bundles are enabled. Unless a rule is explicitly approved, the interface must not imply that bundle credits are transferable, cash-redeemable or refundable as cash. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 8. Career readiness assessment and guided journey ## 8.1 Eight dimensions 1. Resume 2. LinkedIn 3. Portfolio 4. Communication 5. Technical Skills 6. Interview Skills 7. Career Planning 8. Confidence ## 8.2 Segment weights --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Dimension Student Fresh graduate Early career Career switcher --------------- -------------- -------------- -------------- -------------- Resume 15% 15% 15% 15% LinkedIn 8% 10% 10% 10% Portfolio 12% 10% 5% 15% Communication 10% 10% 10% 10% Technical 20% 20% 15% 15% Skills Interview 15% 15% 15% 10% Skills Career Planning 10% 10% 20% 20% Confidence 10% 10% 10% 5% **Total** **100%** **100%** **100%** **100%** --------------------------------------------------------------------------- These are initial approved weights, not empirically validated predictors of hiring outcomes. ## 8.3 Dimension scoring Each dimension uses a 0--5 scale: Score Interpretation ------- ------------------------------------- 0 No evidence / not assessed 1 Major improvement required 2 Below expected level 3 Developing / acceptable foundation 4 Strong 5 Excellent and supported by evidence Overall readiness is displayed from 0--100: `Readiness = Σ(weight × dimension_score / 5) × 100` If Portfolio is not applicable, its weight is redistributed proportionally across applicable dimensions. A missing score must not silently be treated as zero unless the assessment rubric explicitly defines that state. ## 8.4 Evidence and assessment integrity - Store dimension score, rubric version, evidence reference, assessor, assessment date and confidence/coverage. - Confidence is a separate dimension and must not be used as a proxy for technical competence. - Students may provide evidence and request reassessment; mentor/admin updates must be attributable. - Preserve historical assessments rather than overwriting history. - Display evidence coverage and last-assessed date with the score. - The readiness score is private by default. - Do not market the score as a scientifically validated probability of getting hired. ## 8.5 Guided career journey The journey should: - Explain the user's current stage. - Identify the highest-priority gaps. - Recommend a small number of next actions. - Link recommended services to the relevant gap. - Record completed actions and assessment changes. - Avoid arbitrary stage advancement based only on time or purchases. A recommendation must include a reason such as "Technical Skills is currently the highest weighted gap for your selected goal." ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 9. Mentor onboarding, verification and quality ## 9.1 Three separate trust checks 1. **Identity verification:** confirms the applicant is a real person and identity evidence matches. 2. **Professional/experience verification:** reviews work history, claimed expertise, credentials and professional background. 3. **Service-specific approval:** confirms which individual DreamX services the mentor may deliver. A work-email OTP may support professional-domain evidence but does not, by itself, prove identity, current employment or service competence. ## 9.2 Mentor application workflow 1. Create an account as a mentor applicant. 2. Submit profile, experience, target domains and requested services. 3. Complete identity verification using an approved process. 4. Submit professional evidence. 5. Admin/reviewer evaluates the professional background. 6. Each requested service is separately approved, rejected or returned for more evidence. 7. Mentor configures availability and approved pricing. 8. Only eligible approved mentors can accept paid bookings. ## 9.3 Mentor profile fields - Display name and profile photo. - Professional headline and biography. - Current/most relevant professional experience, with verification label. - Skills and career domains. - Approved service list. - Education and credentials where relevant. - Languages. - Years of experience, where verified or clearly self-reported. - Availability and timezone. - Service-specific rating/review information when enough valid data exists. - Clear verification status labels. Do not publish private identity documents, personal contact information or unverified claims as verified facts. ## 9.4 Quality operations Track: - Acceptance and response time. - Attendance and cancellation. - Report delivery SLA. - Student feedback. - Dispute and refund patterns. - Service-specific quality and repeat bookings. Provide review, suspension and appeal workflows. A mentor's approval for one service does not automatically approve every other service. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 10. Discovery, booking and Smart Match ## 10.1 Discovery Students can discover by: - Career goal. - Service category. - Mentor skills and experience. - Availability. - Price. - Approved verification labels. - Rating/reviews where available. Ranking must not imply outcome superiority without valid evidence. Paid placement or sponsored ranking, if introduced later, must be disclosed. ## 10.2 Direct booking 1. Student selects an eligible service and mentor. 2. Student selects an available slot. 3. Server validates mentor approval, slot availability, service price and student eligibility. 4. Slot is held for the configured checkout window. 5. Student completes payment. 6. Verified payment event confirms booking. 7. Calendar/in-app/email confirmations are dispatched. 8. If payment fails or expires, the hold is released safely. The database must enforce atomic slot claiming to prevent double booking. The client cannot set booking status to confirmed. ## 10.3 Smart Match Smart Match is a deterministic MVP workflow, not an LLM-based matching feature. - Match only mentors approved for the requested service and available for the requested windows. - Consider service fit, career-domain fit, availability, experience/verification, quality signals and operational load. - Store match reasons and the matching configuration/version. - Student submits preferred time windows and the request. - Initial dispatch wave starts when the request is valid and eligible. - **Wave 2 begins after 30 minutes** if no mentor has accepted. - If no mentor accepts within **2 hours**, DreamX presents an available open-slot alternative or a full refund option. - The student explicitly chooses the alternative or refund. - DreamX must not automatically reschedule the student. - Mentor acceptance must use an atomic claim so only one mentor can win the booking. - Notify the student when a mentor accepts and make the confirmed details visible. - If the provider's payment model cannot support the described hold/refund path, the flow must be adapted before launch and documented. ## 10.4 No double booking and race conditions Acceptance and slot reservation must be transaction-safe. Two mentors accepting the same request simultaneously must result in exactly one successful acceptance. A losing mentor receives a clear "already accepted" response. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 11. Session delivery, reports and outcomes ## 11.1 Session lifecycle - Booking confirmed. - Pre-session information collected. - Reminder dispatched. - Student and mentor join. - Attendance events recorded. - Session completed or exception recorded. - Mentor submits the service-specific report. - Report reviewed/approved if the service or risk policy requires it. - Student receives report and action plan. - Readiness history and next-step recommendations update when appropriate. ## 11.2 Reports - A report is required for each service where the catalogue promises one. - Target report delivery: **within 48 hours after the session**. - Report structure varies by service; do not force every service into the same scorecard. - Reports must include the service performed, key observations, actionable recommendations and next steps. - Reports should identify the author and creation/version time. - Corrections/revisions must preserve prior versions and an audit trail. - A late report triggers a mentor reminder and an operations queue. - PDF and secure web view are acceptable delivery formats; final format selection belongs in UX/technical specifications. ## 11.3 Outcome tracking Students may voluntarily record milestones such as resume completion, applications, interviews, shortlists and offers. Outcome data must be labelled as self-reported unless independently verified. Public aggregate claims require sufficient sample size, clear denominator, timeframe and methodology. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 12. Cancellation, rescheduling, refunds and disputes ## 12.1 Approved baseline - Student cancellation at least **1 hour before** a 1:1 session: **100% refund**. - Post-session issue report: submit within **48 hours** after the session. - If a post-session issue is substantiated under the published policy: **80% refund**. - If the issue is not substantiated: provide a reasoned decision and evidence summary, subject to applicable consumer rights. - Refund initiation and refund completion are separate states. - Never claim that a refund is instant unless the payment provider confirms that timeline. ## 12.2 Operational handling defaults requiring final policy publication The following should be treated as operational defaults and reviewed before launch: ----------------------------------------------------------------------- Scenario Recommended handling ----------------------------------- ----------------------------------- Student cancels at least 1 hour Full refund under the approved before baseline Student cancels less than 1 hour Normally no automatic refund; allow before documented exception review Student no-show Normally no refund if mentor attended and was available for the stated grace period; evidence and legal review required Mentor cancels Student chooses a suitable reschedule or full refund Mentor no-show Full refund or student-selected reschedule; no automatic reschedule Late arrival Record join times; apply a published grace/shortened-session rule Technical failure Determine responsibility using platform/attendance evidence; provide reschedule or refund as appropriate Report not delivered within 48 Operations escalation; require hours delivery or issue resolution Quality complaint after session Student submits within 48 hours; review evidence and apply substantiated 80% refund rule ----------------------------------------------------------------------- The exact grace period, late-arrival treatment, reschedule limits, and student/mentor no-show definitions must be published in the final customer and mentor terms before paid launch. ## 12.3 Dispute evidence Evidence may include: - Booking and payment events. - Attendance timestamps. - Session schedule and approved service scope. - In-platform communications. - Report submission timestamps and report versions. - User-submitted supporting information. No session recording is stored by default. Transcripts or recordings may only be considered after explicit informed consent from all participants, legal review, purpose limitation, access controls and a defined retention policy. A transcript must never be the sole automated judge of a dispute. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 13. Payments, fees and mentor settlement ## 13.1 Financial principles - All money is stored as integer paise. - Client-supplied prices and payout amounts are never authoritative. - Payment status is based on verified provider events and reconciliation. - Webhooks must be signature-verified, idempotent and replay-safe. - Payment capture, booking confirmation, refund and mentor settlement are distinct states. - Maintain a complete financial audit trail. ## 13.2 Marketplace payout model **LAUNCH BLOCKER --- provider and legal validation required.** Do not assume: - Razorpay Route is approved for DreamX. - Split settlement is available to DreamX. - Escrow or on-hold transfers are permitted. - DreamX may collect and later transfer funds under an unreviewed arrangement. - Any specific payout percentage, settlement cadence or KYC flow is provider-approved. The previously discussed mentor payout model based on original list price while DreamX absorbs discounts is the intended commercial direction, but final commission, gateway fee treatment, taxes, settlement timing, refund deductions and payout statements require provider and legal confirmation. The system must isolate provider-specific operations behind an adapter so the approved provider can be selected without rewriting core booking logic. If no compliant marketplace collection and settlement model is available, paid marketplace launch is blocked. Do not create a custom "escrow" wallet as a workaround. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 14. Analytics and product measurement ## 20.1 Core funnel - Visitor → signup. - Signup → onboarding completion. - Onboarding → assessment completion. - Assessment → service recommendation viewed. - Recommendation → booking initiated. - Booking initiated → payment success. - Payment success → session completed. - Session completed → report delivered within 48 hours. - Report delivered → next action completed / repeat booking. ## 20.2 Marketplace health Track: - Approved mentors by service. - Available slots and mentor utilisation. - Direct booking conversion. - Smart Match acceptance and fulfilment. - Time to mentor acceptance. - Cancellation and no-show rates. - Payment failure and refund rates. - Report SLA compliance. - Dispute rates and resolution time. - Repeat booking and bundle use. - Membership activation, renewal and churn. - Readiness assessment completion and evidence coverage. ## 20.3 Measurement rules - Define every metric's numerator, denominator, event time and exclusions. - Separate self-reported outcomes from verified outcomes. - Avoid publishing small-sample outcome percentages. - Do not optimise for booking volume at the expense of quality, refunds or mentor wellbeing. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 21. Acceptance criteria and definition of done A feature is not complete merely because its UI exists. ## 21.1 Core acceptance criteria ### Identity and roles - A student cannot self-promote to mentor or admin. - Mentor applicants cannot accept paid bookings before required verification and service approval. - Admin actions are permission-checked and audited. ### Readiness - Segment weights total 100%. - Score calculation is deterministic and versioned. - Not-applicable dimensions are handled according to the documented redistribution rule. - History is preserved and score visibility defaults to private. ### Catalogue and checkout - Service duration, list price and deliverable are visible before purchase. - Final price is calculated server-side. - Discounts do not stack. - Money uses integer paise. ### Booking and Smart Match - Unavailable slots cannot be booked. - Concurrent acceptance cannot create two confirmed bookings. - Wave 2 begins at 30 minutes if no mentor accepts. - Two-hour timeout presents an open-slot alternative or full refund option. - No reschedule occurs without student choice. ### Payment - Invalid/replayed webhooks cannot create duplicate payment or booking events. - Booking confirmation follows verified payment state. - Refund initiation and completion are separately represented. - Provider reconciliation is available. ### Reports - Applicable service reports are delivered within 48 hours or escalated. - Report versions and timestamps are preserved. - Student can access only their own report. ### Privacy and security - RLS tests cover cross-student and cross-mentor access. - Private files are not publicly accessible. - Sensitive data is excluded from logs. - Deletion and retention workflows are testable. ## 21.2 Definition of done Each feature must have: 1. Phase 2 --- AI tools and credit economy This section defines the approved Phase 2 commercial baseline. It is not an MVP requirement and must not be implemented in Phase 1 unless separately approved. ## 25.1 Credit conversion and pricing - Preferred conversion: **₹1 = 10 credits**. - Every AI feature has a visible, fixed credit price. - Feature prices are configurable and versioned. - Reserve credits when a task starts. - Settle the reservation on successful completion. - Release/refund the reservation on failure or timeout. - Track actual model/provider cost separately from user credits. ## 25.2 Monthly allowances Plan Monthly allowance ------- ------------------- Free 150 credits Pro 500 credits Elite 1,000 credits Monthly allowances expire at the end of the membership cycle and do not roll over. ## 25.3 Credit packs Pack Price Base credits --------- -------- -------------- Starter ₹50 500 Basic ₹100 1,000 Value ₹250 2,500 Power ₹500 5,500 Large ₹1,000 12,000 Bonus credits: - Free: 0%. - Pro: 5%. - Elite: 10%. Purchased base credits do not expire. Bonus credits expire after 90 days. Credits are granted only after verified payment; webhook processing must be idempotent. ## 25.4 Cashback credits - Free: 0%. - Pro: 5%. - Elite: 10%. - Cashback is calculated on the original listed service price before discounts. - Issue after service completion and 48 hours without a complaint, or after dispute resolution. - Full refund reverses all related cashback; partial refund reverses proportionally. - One cashback grant per booking. - Cashback is non-cash, non-transferable and non-withdrawable. - Cashback expires after 90 days. ## 25.5 Spend order and wallet integrity - Spend credits by earliest expiry first. - For equal expiry, spend the oldest issued credits first. - Protect non-expiring purchased base credits from accidental expiry. - Use atomic reservations and deductions. - No negative wallet balances. - Maintain an immutable credit ledger. ## 25.6 Fair use and cost controls - Configurable daily limits by plan and feature. - Input, output, concurrency and frequency limits. - Cost alerts at 70%, 85% and 95% of configured budget thresholds. - Admin may pause a costly or unsafe feature. - Track quality, latency, failure rate, cost per successful task and user feedback. - AI outputs must be labelled as AI-assisted and provide appropriate limitations. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ # 26.
